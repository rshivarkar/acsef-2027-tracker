Fair rules reminder — read before every work session
  • Low voltage only (3×AA battery packs).
  • Nothing wired to the furnace, AC or household power.
  • No testing on people — temperature targets only.
  • No heaters, motors, chemicals or lasers.
  • Say "detect", never "diagnose".
  • Students write the Research Plan, abstract, poster and notebook in their own words.
  • Any AI use must be logged with date, prompt and purpose, and cited (use the AI Use Log below).
  • No Research Plan changes on or after Feb 1, 2027.
  • Adults (Parent) help with safety-related tasks only.
